# 💰 A Comparative Corporate Treasury Analysis: Wipro vs HCL Technologies

**Evaluating Cash Preservation vs. Capital Expenditure Strategies, FY2023 – FY2026** 📊

✍️ By Pallavi A and S Vinay Narasimha · 📅 November 2026 · ⚖️ Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE)

## 🔎 Overview

This project compares how Wipro and HCL Technologies (HCLTech) managed their cash and capital spending over four financial years (years ended 31 March 2023 to 2026). It asks whether either company followed a cash-preservation strategy, a capital-investment strategy, or a mix of both, and lets the financial data decide.

All figures are consolidated, in ₹ crore unless stated, and taken from company filings. Every figure in the original working data was checked against those filings. Many were corrected, and the corrections are documented.

## 📐 Metrics analysed

- Cash-to-Assets
- Cash and liquid investments (gross cash) to Assets
- CapEx-to-Revenue
- Other Income-to-Net Profit (a comparison indicator, not a measure of interest contribution)
- Interest Income-to-Net Profit (where available)
- Revenue, cash balance, gross cash and CapEx growth
- Shareholder payout (dividends and buybacks relative to profit)
- Net cash (gross cash less borrowings)

## 🏆 Headline findings

- 🏦 **Both companies are cash-rich and capital-light.** In FY2026, cash and liquid investments were 38.9% of assets at Wipro and 28.8% at HCLTech (company-defined gross cash). CapEx was 1.7% of revenue at Wipro and 1.1% at HCLTech.
- 🏗️ **Neither company froze investment.** Both cut CapEx sharply in FY2024 and rebuilt it by FY2026.
- 📈 **Growth is where they differ most.** Revenue grew about 8.7% a year at HCLTech against about 0.8% a year at Wipro between FY2023 and FY2026.
- 💼 **Wipro's profit leans more on treasury-type income.** Other income was 27.5% of net profit in FY2026, against 9.2% at HCLTech.
- 🤝 **Shareholder returns differ in form.** HCLTech returned 89.7% of cumulative profit, mostly as dividends. Wipro returned 73.8% through dividends and a buyback.
- 💵 **Net cash** was ₹38,394 crore at Wipro and ₹33,288 crore at HCLTech in FY2026.

The report weighs liquidity, profitability, growth, investment efficiency and shareholder returns together before reaching a balanced conclusion.

## 🧪 Method in brief

- **Sources:** audited consolidated statements, results releases and annual-report filings. No figure was estimated. Unavailable data is marked "Not available".
- **Accounting bases:** Wipro is analysed under IFRS and HCLTech under Ind AS and company releases. Both are consolidated, with a 31 March year-end.
- **Definitions:** cash and cash equivalents are kept separate from liquid investments. CapEx is the cash purchase of property, plant and equipment (HCLTech's cash-flow line also includes intangibles). Net profit is profit for the year including non-controlling interest.
- **Verification:** of 48 original data points compared, 27 were corrected, 18 were verified as correct and 3 remain flagged "Requires further verification" (see the Verification Log).

## ⚠️ Limitations

- HCLTech's ₹ note-level interest income was not extracted. Where available, HCLTech's interest income is taken from its IFRS statements in US dollars.
- IFRS (Wipro) and Ind AS (HCLTech) differences are not reconciled.
- CapEx and gross-cash definitions differ slightly between the two companies.
- HCLTech's FY2025 other income and its FY2023–24 net profit basis are flagged for further verification.
- Some management details (for example earnings-call figures and announced plans) rely on company releases or secondary coverage and are marked as such in the report.
- Four years is a short period spanning a high-interest-rate phase, soft IT demand and the start of the AI shift.
